Our Process

Six considered steps.
One beautifully finished space.

  1. Share your drawings, project location and target timeline.

  2. Review site measurements, finishes and carpentry details together.

  3. Agree on the scope of work and quotation before production begins.

  4. Your confirmed details guide the fabrication of each carpentry piece.

  5. Coordinate site access, delivery and installation with your project team.

  6. Review the installed carpentry and finishing details together at handover.
